## ⚡ Active & Upcoming Feature Roadmap (v1.7 - v2.0)

### 1. ☕ Keep Awake / Caffeine Mode (PowerToys Awake Style)
* **Status:** ⏳ *Planned for v1.7*
* **Description:** 1-click temporary sleep & display timeout inhibitor in the System Tray and Title Bar.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Preset durations: `30 mins`, `1 hour`, `2 hours`, `Indefinite`, or `Until active process finishes` (e.g. large downloads, video rendering, exports).
  * Implemented using native Win32 `SetThreadExecutionState` (`ES_CONTINUOUS | ES_SYSTEM_REQUIRED | ES_DISPLAY_REQUIRED`) without altering permanent Windows Power Plan configurations.
  * System tray quick toggle with coffee cup icon state change and tooltip remaining timer.
* **Reference:** [Microsoft PowerToys Awake](https://github.com/microsoft/PowerToys)

---

### 2. 🎯 Floating Hardware HUD & Compact In-Game Overlay
* **Status:** ⏳ *Planned for v1.7*
* **Description:** Minimalist, semi-transparent hardware vitals pill/overlay for desktop and gaming.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Real-time metrics: CPU/GPU temps, clock speeds, active Wattage draw (e.g., `18.5W`), battery drain rate, and active Power Mode.
  * Customizable position (Corner snap / draggable) and global toggle hotkey (e.g., `Ctrl + Shift + O`).
  * Low CPU overhead using non-polling Windows WMI / Performance Counters / LibreHardwareMonitor library.
* **Reference:** [LibreHardwareMonitor](https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor)

---

### 3. ⌨️ Global Hotkeys & Screen OSD Mode Flyout
* **Status:** ⏳ *Planned for v1.7*
* **Description:** Keyboard shortcuts to cycle power modes on the fly with a modern Windows 11 Fluent On-Screen Display (OSD) notification.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Configurable hotkey (e.g., `Win + Shift + P` or `Fn + Q` on supported laptops).
  * Centered translucent pop-up banner showing current mode transition: `🍃 Eco Mode` ➔ `⚡ Smart Mode` ➔ `🚀 Beast Mode` ➔ `🎮 Game Mode`.
  * Audio chirp feedback option on switch.

---

### 4. 🌙 Modern Standby Fix & Deep Sleep Auto-Hibernate
* **Status:** ⏳ *Planned for v1.8*
* **Description:** Prevents laptop battery drain and backpack overheating caused by Windows 11 Modern Standby (S0) bugs.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Auto-transition from Sleep to Hibernate after a user-defined period on battery (e.g. 15 or 30 minutes).
  * Auto-disables network connectivity during Modern Standby to eliminate battery drain while asleep.
  * Sleep battery drain diagnostics tool (analyzing Windows SleepStudy logs via `powercfg /sleepstudy`).

### 7. ⏱️ Next-Gen Game Mode Latency Engine (Deep System Tuning)
* **Status:** ⏳ *Planned for v1.8 - v2.0*
* **Description:** Advanced esports-tier system latency optimizations for the Game Mode engine.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * **0.5ms System Timer Resolution:** Temporarily call native `NtSetTimerResolution` to force ultra-low input latency for competitive FPS titles.
  * **CPU Core Pinning & Affinity:** Bind game threads strictly to Performance cores (Intel 12th-14th Gen P-Cores) or cache-heavy CCD (AMD Ryzen 7000/9000X3D CPUs).
  * **DirectX GPU Scheduling & Priority:** Set foreground game process scheduling priority to `High` / `Real-Time` with DirectX GPU preemption.
  * **Windows Focus Assist & DND Auto-Trigger:** Automatically silence Windows 11 notifications, banners, and suppress background driver installs during active gameplay.

### 8. 🪟 Advanced Winhance Deep Integrations (memstechtips Blueprint)
* **Status:** ⏳ *Planned for v1.8 - v2.0*
* **Description:** Advanced system enhancement, transparency, unattended deployment, and persistent update protection inspired by [Winhance](https://github.com/memstechtips/Winhance).
* **Key Capabilities:**

  * **AxePower Fresh-Start Builder:** Export configured settings and power plans into a standardized `autounattend.xml` answer file for zero-touch clean Windows installations.
  * **Dynamic DWM Visual Scaling:** Auto-disable window animations and acrylic blurs on Eco Mode, restore Fluent aesthetics on Smart/Beast Mode.
* **Reference:** [Winhance (memstechtips/Winhance)](https://github.com/memstechtips/Winhance) & [winhance.net](https://winhance.net)

### 10. 🎵 Compact Music Player & Universal Media HUD (Spotify / Apple Music / Hi-Res Lossless / Local)
* **Status:** ⏳ *Planned for v1.8*
* **Description:** Ultra-lightweight background music player, universal media controller widget, and audiophile-grade lossless streaming hub built directly into the AxePower Tray, Floating HUD, and Remote Phone Dashboard.
* **Key Capabilities & Synergies with AxePower:**
  * **Direct Streaming Account Integrations (Spotify, Apple Music & YouTube Music):**
    * **Spotify Connect & Web API (OAuth2 PKCE):** Sign in to Spotify account to browse personal playlists, Liked Songs, Daily Mixes, and gaming playlists directly in AxePower. Seamless device target switching via Spotify Connect (transfer playback between PC, phone, or smart speakers) without needing the resource-heavy Spotify desktop app open.
    * **Apple Music (MusicKit Integration):** Connect Apple Music account to access personal iCloud Music Library, curated playlists, Apple Music 1 live radio, and high-fidelity audio streams.
    * **YouTube Music & Tidal:** Connect accounts to fetch user playlists, artist radios, and personal libraries.
    * **Discord Rich Presence & Last.fm Scrobbler:** Dynamic "Listening to [Track] by [Artist]" Discord activity status with album cover art and automatic track scrobbling to Last.fm.
    * **Synchronized Live Lyrics:** On-screen timed karaoke lyrics in the in-game overlay or mobile dashboard.
  * **Audiophile-Grade Hi-Res Lossless Audio Engine:**
    * **Bit-Perfect Playback (WASAPI Exclusive & ASIO):** Direct hardware-level audio streaming bypassing the Windows audio mixer (DWM/AudioSrv resampling and DSP) directly to external USB DACs, gaming soundcards, and high-end headphone amps for pristine audio fidelity.
    * **Comprehensive Lossless Codec Support:** Native bit-perfect playback for **FLAC**, **ALAC** (Apple Lossless), **DSD** (DSF/DFF via DoP), **WAV**, **AIFF**, **APE** (Monkey's Audio), and high-bitrate Opus/OGG up to 24-bit/192kHz and 32-bit/384kHz.
    * **Lossless Cloud Streaming Quality:** Native support for Apple Music Lossless / Hi-Res Lossless (ALAC), Tidal HiFi / Max (FLAC 24-bit/192kHz), and Spotify Lossless ready.
    * **Live Audio Stream Badge:** HUD badge displaying real-time audio quality (e.g. `[Hi-Res Lossless 24-bit/96kHz FLAC]`, `[Bit-Perfect WASAPI Exclusive]`, `[Apple Lossless ALAC]`).
    * **Automatic DAC Sample Rate Switching:** Matches external DAC hardware clock frequency to source audio sample rate to eliminate software re-sampling jitter.
  * **Universal Windows SMTC Integration:** Hooks into Windows 10/11 `GlobalSystemMediaTransportControlsSessionManager` as a universal fallback to detect and control any active media playing on the PC (Spotify app, Apple Music, YouTube in browsers, VLC, Foobar2000, Tidal).
  * **Floating / In-Game Mini Player Widget:**
    * Sleek Fluent translucent pill or compact corner overlay showing live album art, track title, artist, lossless quality badge, animated audio visualizer bar, seekbar, playlist picker, and volume wheel.
    * Transparent click-through or hotkey-toggled (`Ctrl + Shift + M`) in-game HUD overlay that doesn't steal focus from fullscreen games.
  * **Built-in Low-Overhead Local & Ambient Audio Player:**
    * Embedded lightweight audio engine (`NAudio` or `ManagedBass`) for playing local lossless audio with <15 MB RAM footprint—avoiding heavy Electron apps or browser tabs.
    * Curated gamer & focus soundboard: Built-in Lo-Fi chill streams, synthwave radio, rain/storm, and pink/white noise generators for gaming and productivity sessions.
  * **Smart Game & Power Automation:**
    * **Auto-Pause / Duck on Game Launch:** Automatically pause or duck background music volume when entering competitive games or active voice calls (Discord).
    * **Eco-Friendly Playback:** Hardware-accelerated audio offloading (`WASAPI`) to minimize CPU wakeups and preserve laptop battery during extended playback.
  * **Seamless Phone Remote & Tray Integration:** Full account playlist browsing, playback controls, live track info, lossless bitrate indicators, and volume controls mirrored directly onto the mobile browser dashboard (`http://<local-ip>:5050`) and the Steam-style tray menu.
* **Reference:** [Spotify Web API / Spotify Connect](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api), [Apple MusicKit](https://developer.apple.com/musickit/), [NAudio WASAPI Exclusive](https://github.com/naudio/NAudio), [Windows Media SMTC API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/uwp/api/windows.media.control.globalsystemmediatransportcontrolssessionmanager)
