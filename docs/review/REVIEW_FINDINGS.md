## How to record a finding

One entry per finding, in the reviewer's words. Then the owner adds the triage.

```
### R<n> — <title>
**Reviewer / date:** <organisation or participant number> / <date>
**Source:** usability session | security assessment
**Severity (reviewer):** <as reported>     **Severity (owner):** <after triage>
**What happened:** <steps, evidence, quote>
**Triage:** confirmed | duplicate of <id> | not reproduced | won't fix (why)
**Task:** <link to the tracked fix, once confirmed>
```

Ids `R<n>` are never reused. A confirmed finding becomes a tracked fix: a numbered task in
`SOFTWARE_COMPLETION_PROMPTS.md` or the relevant fix plan, linked here. It stays here after it is
fixed, with the fixing commit, because this file is the record of what reviewers found.
