Privacy Policy

What this website, the AxePower desktop app and the AxePower phone app do with your data.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. This website

If you allow it, this website uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. Nothing is loaded and no analytics cookies are set until you choose “Accept” in the cookie banner. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and collects information such as the pages you view, how you arrived, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Advertising features are turned off. This information is processed by Google under its own privacy policy; we use it only in aggregate to improve the site, and never to identify you or for advertising.

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings (also in every page's footer); choosing “Reject” removes the analytics cookies. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, analytics stays off unless you turn it on yourself. Your choice is stored only in your browser. The site works the same either way.

Separately from analytics, the company that hosts this website keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address, the page requested and the time, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

Links to downloads, source code, community spaces and donation services take you to other websites, which have their own privacy policies. If you donate, the payment service you choose handles the payment and shares only what it tells you it shares with the recipient.

2. The AxePower desktop app

The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or usage tracking. It does not send data about you or how you use it to its developers, and it does not sell or share your data.

Stored on your computer: your settings, power profiles, themes and automations; music data such as library folders, playlists, recently played songs, recent searches and the saved queue; activity logs used to diagnose problems; and backups that some tools make before changing your system. These are kept in the app-data folders of your Windows user profile and never leave your computer on their own. Deleting those folders removes them.

Online features. Some features contact third-party services directly from your computer, and only when you use them:

  • searching and streaming music, which sends your search text and the song being played;
  • finding lyrics, which sends the song's title, artist and length;
  • checking for AxePower updates, and updating software or drivers when you ask it to;
  • links, downloads and help pages, which open in your web browser.

Like any website, these services also see your IP address and the request itself, and their own privacy policies and terms apply to what they receive. AxePower does not route this traffic through any server of its own.

Your devices and connections. Phone control, file transfer, remote playback control and screen sharing work over your local network or an address you choose to connect to, and data goes directly between those devices. Local-network access is protected by a private link shared by QR code, and nothing is shared until you start it.

System access. The app runs with administrator rights so it can change power, performance and system settings. It changes your system only when you choose an action or set up an automation. Some changes cannot always be fully undone, so read each prompt before confirming.

3. The AxePower phone app

The phone app talks only to your own PC, over your local Wi-Fi or a USB connection. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no connection to any cloud service.

4. Open-source components and third-party tools

AxePower is built with open-source libraries and can install, launch or work alongside third-party tools. Each remains the property of its authors and is used under its own license; the full license texts are included with the app in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, next to AxePower.exe. Any tool you launch through AxePower is covered by that tool's own terms and privacy policy. AxePower is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of them. All product names, logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

5. Children

AxePower and this website are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. For authors and rights holders

If you are the author or owner of any software, content or trademark that AxePower uses or refers to, and you would like a credit added, corrected or removed, please contact us or open an issue on GitHub. We respect creators' rights and will act on such requests promptly.

7. Changes and contact

If the way AxePower or this website handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's GitHub issues.
