About AxePower

Built out of the frustration with proprietary bloatware, battery drain, and thermal throttling on Windows laptops and gaming rigs.

⚡ Our Mission

AxePower was created to give PC users a single, clean, beautiful control center that automates power management, eliminates micro-stutters in games, and provides wireless companion tools without ads or spyware.

We believe software should respect user privacy, run with near-zero CPU overhead, and operate 100% locally on your machine.

🔒 100% Local & Privacy-First

AxePower collects zero telemetry, tracks zero personal data, and makes zero external network calls other than checking for verified GitHub releases and your own local Wi-Fi companion sync.

Your settings, hardware diagnostics, and preferences remain solely on your hard drive.

👤 Developer

Developed with dedication by Abhimanyu Vishwakarma. Built using .NET 8 WPF, C# 12, MVVM architecture, CommunityToolkit.Mvvm, and native Windows APIs.
