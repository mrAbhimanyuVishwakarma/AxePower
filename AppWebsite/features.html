Intelligent Power Engine

Automatically toggles system power plans and display refresh rates (60Hz vs 144/165/240Hz) based on AC power vs Battery state.

  • ✓Eco Mode: Capped power states to extend battery runtime on the go.
  • ✓Smart Mode: Dynamic scaling for everyday work and video streaming.
  • ✓Beast Mode: Max CPU/GPU clocks and sustained thermal power.

Game Mode & Multi-Drive Scanner

Scans all fixed drives (C:, D:, E:, etc.) to detect running games instantly, lock display refresh rates, and suspend background bloat.

Sortable Uninstaller & Leftover Cleaner
